## 1. Brand Philosophy: The Independent Studio Manifesto
The MWAEL portfolio is conceived not as a transactional resume or developer showcase, but as an **interactive design monograph** produced by a high-end independent creative engineering studio.

### Core Creative Identity:
```
EDITORIAL MINIMALISM
+ ATMOSPHERIC LIGHTING (#100e0b Canvas + 6-Layer Aurora)
+ DIGITAL CHARACTER (Expressive 2D Vector Kinematics)
+ CINEMATIC STORYTELLING (Pinned & Masked Sequential Reveals)
```

The visitor experience balances **computational discipline** (C++, Python, AI agent architectures) with **high-end visual taste** (typographic restraint, organic motion, architectural whitespace).

- **Monospace Metadata**: `#00f5d4` / `#a78bfa` — Aurora-inspired cyan and soft violet for technical tags, dates, and live coordinates.

## 3. Typography Architecture

### Editorial Display:
- **Tone**: High-contrast, classical editorial presence paired with technological precision.
- **Hierarchy**:
  - **Mega Title (Hero)**: Fluid `clamp(3.5rem, 8vw, 7.5rem)` with tight tracking (`-0.04em`), uppercase/italic contrast, and split-line mask reveals.
  - **Section Headings**: `clamp(2rem, 5vw, 4.25rem)` with numbered index prefixes (`01`, `02`, `03`).
  - **Project Titles**: `clamp(1.75rem, 3.5vw, 3rem)` with weight contrast between client name and domain discipline.

### Swiss Precision Sans & Monospace:
- **Body Text**: Clean geometric sans-serif at `1rem` (16px) with generous line-height (`1.65`) for effortless readability.
- **Technical Taxonomy & Meta**: Monospaced font at `0.75rem`–`0.85rem` with uppercase tracking (`0.1em`) for coordinates, live time tickers, and code frameworks.

## 4. Layout Architecture & Whitespace Philosophy
1. **Asymmetrical Grid Composition**:
   - Avoid repetitive three-column card grids.
   - Utilize 12-column editorial grids where media and typography occupy varying spans (e.g., 7-column media hero paired with a 5-column sticky narrative).
2. **Disciplined Negative Space**:
   - Generous section padding (`py-28 md:py-44`).
   - Content breathes naturally, directing focus to one high-impact idea or case study at a time.
3. **No Artificial UI Clichés**:
   - Zero generic rainbow gradients or neon button borders.
   - Zero oversized floating cards with 24px rounded corners.
   - Zero fake progress bars or arbitrary percentage circles (e.g., "Python 92%").
   - Every skill, technology, and milestone links directly to verified academic and professional evidence.

- **SiteFooter**: Monogram stamp, copyright, and playful interactive character farewell.
